Find the companies worth researching.

NPSE helps you decide which Nepal-listed companies deserve a closer look, what price and evidence would justify an entry, and what would change your mind. It separates business quality, valuation, timing and data confidence. An empty shortlist is a valid result when evidence is missing.

Why each page is here

Decision Board and Candidates

See up to three qualified companies and why each passes, waits or fails. Coverage and freshness panels show what evidence the shortlist is based on.

Sectors and Company research

Compare similar businesses. Company panels show bear, base and bull valuations when supported, along with evidence, entry conditions and what would invalidate a thesis.

Daily Evidence, Sources and Methodology

Trace what changed and how the engine reached a result. Keep dated company events, source authority and calculation rules visible.

Diagnostics and Backtests

Inspect prices, volume, data gaps and test readiness separately from investment qualification.

How to use and Workspace

Learn the daily routine, export current evidence and find the working Drive files. Editing a research document does not change the board automatically.
